# Badge

Frosted pill with a slowly spinning snowflake. It sits above the hero headline and states the company's experience.

## Markup
```html
<span class="kk-badge kk-frost">
  <img src="flake-blue.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" class="kk-spin">
  W chłodnictwie od 1989 roku · Wrocław
</span>
```

## The consumer provides
- One short line of text, max ~45 characters.
- The `flake-blue.svg` motif (assets › Motifs).

## Rules
- One badge per page, above the h1.
- 1989 is when the family's refrigeration experience began. Klima-Kar itself started in 1999. Never call 1989 the founding year.

# Gallery

Four 4:5 photos in frost frames, with every second tile dropped 48px for a staggered rhythm.

## The consumer provides
- 4 photos (8 on the /galeria page) with descriptive Polish `alt` text naming the car or the place.

## Rules
- Real workshop photos only, in natural colour. No duotone, no stock.
- Mobile: 2 columns, stagger 28px.
- On /galeria, wrap the grid in the same SectionHeader and repeat `kk-g4` rows.

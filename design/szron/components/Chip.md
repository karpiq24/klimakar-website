# Chip

Small pill used for tags and for live status. It comes in variants for light, brand and navy grounds.

## Variants
| Class | Ground | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `kk-chip kk-chip--tag` | frost, `ground` | Service tags on partner cards ("Montaż", "Naprawa") |
| `kk-chip kk-chip--now` + `kk-dot` | anywhere light | "Teraz" on the current season |
| `kk-chip kk-chip--on-brand` + `kk-dot` | `brand` card | Live status ("Od ręki") |
| `kk-chip kk-chip--ice` + `kk-dot` | spotlight (navy) card | Distinction ("Nieliczni w Polsce") |
| `kk-chip kk-chip--outline` | spotlight card | Facts ("R744 · CO₂", "Pompy ciepła") |

## Rules
- The pulsing `kk-dot` means "true right now". Use it only for live status, at most one per card.
- Chip text is 13px; keep it to 1–3 words.
